## **1. Problem Statement**

The event management industry continues to operate through fragmented and largely offline processes, despite the increasing demand for professionally managed weddings, corporate events, cultural programs, birthdays, anniversaries, and other celebrations. Clients, event management teams, and event professionals still rely heavily on referrals, social media, spreadsheets, phone calls, and manual coordination, resulting in inefficiencies throughout the event lifecycle.

From a client's perspective, discovering and evaluating suitable event management teams remains challenging. There is no standardized platform to compare planners based on their expertise, services, pricing, portfolios, and event concepts before making a booking decision. As a result, clients often face uncertainty regarding event quality, design expectations, budgets, and overall service transparency.

Event management teams face a broader operational challenge beyond acquiring clients. Managing inquiries, preparing proposals, visualizing event concepts, coordinating vendors, recruiting skilled professionals, tracking ongoing events, and handling day-to-day business operations are often performed using disconnected tools and manual processes. This increases administrative effort, reduces operational efficiency, and limits business scalability.

Similarly, event professionals—including photographers, videographers, designers, decorators, coordinators, freelancers, interns, and other specialists—lack a dedicated platform to showcase their skills, discover project-based opportunities, and connect directly with event management teams. Recruitment continues to depend largely on personal networks rather than a structured talent marketplace.

As a result, client acquisition, proposal management, workforce recruitment, and business operations remain fragmented across multiple disconnected platforms and manual workflows. The industry lacks a unified technology platform that enables event businesses to acquire clients, create winning proposals, recruit skilled professionals, and efficiently manage their day-to-day operations through a single integrated ecosystem.

## **2. Proposed Solution**

Celebrate is an **AI-powered Operating System for the Event Industry** that unifies **Clients**, **Event Management Teams**, and **Event Professionals** within a single intelligent ecosystem. The platform digitalizes the complete event lifecycle—from client acquisition and proposal generation to workforce recruitment and business operations—through four integrated platforms that work together seamlessly.

### **1. Client Acquisition Platform**

Clients create detailed event requirements by specifying the event type, budget, location, venue details, guest count, preferences, and optional reference images. Celebrate's **AI-powered Smart Planner Matching Engine** semantically analyzes these requirements and identifies the most suitable event management teams based on their expertise, services, portfolio, operating locations, and budget compatibility. Only highly relevant planners receive the event request, enabling clients to receive high-quality, personalized proposals.

### **2. AI Proposal Platform**

Matched event management teams prepare customized proposals that include event execution strategies, budget estimates, service offerings, venue recommendations (where applicable), and AI-assisted visual concepts of the event setup using the **AI Design Canvas**. Clients can compare multiple proposals, review realistic event designs, collaborate on revisions, and confidently select the planner that best aligns with their expectations.

### **3. Event Professional Marketplace**

Celebrate provides a dedicated recruitment marketplace where photographers, videographers, decorators, designers, coordinators, freelancers, interns, and other event professionals can build verified portfolios, showcase their skills, discover nearby opportunities, and apply for project-based roles. Event management teams can simultaneously recruit suitable professionals using intelligent search, recommendation, and matching capabilities, enabling them to efficiently build event-specific teams.

### **4. Business Operations Platform**

Beyond client acquisition and workforce recruitment, Celebrate serves as a centralized business operations platform for event management teams. The platform enables planners to manage client relationships, proposals, events, team members, recruitment, payments, documents, calendars, task assignments, analytics, and other day-to-day business operations from a single integrated workspace. By consolidating operational workflows into one platform, Celebrate helps event businesses improve efficiency, scalability, and long-term growth.

Together, these four integrated platforms transform Celebrate from a conventional event marketplace into a comprehensive operating system that empowers event businesses to acquire clients, win projects, recruit skilled professionals, and manage their complete business operations through one connected technology ecosystem.

## **3. Key Scenarios**

### **Scenario 1: Client Has a Predefined Venue**

The client has already finalized the event venue and provides venue details along with event requirements. Event management teams receive the request, create customized proposals, and use the AI Design Canvas to visualize décor and event concepts based on the selected venue.

### **Scenario 2: Client Has Not Finalized a Venue**

The client specifies the event type, budget, preferred location, and requirements without selecting a venue. Event management teams recommend suitable venues within the client's budget and submit complete proposals that include venue options, event designs, budgeting, and execution plans.

### **Scenario 3: Planner may even build a new Event Team**

After a proposal is accepted, the event management team recruits photographers, videographers, decorators, coordinators, freelancers, interns, and other professionals through the Event Professional Marketplace. Celebrate streamlines workforce discovery, recruitment, and collaboration for successful event execution.

### **Scenario 4: Planner Manages Business Operations**

Throughout the event lifecycle, the event management team uses Celebrate's Business Operations Platform to manage client communication, proposals, team assignments, payments, schedules, documents, and event progress from a centralized workspace, ensuring efficient execution and long-term business management.

- Validate the Smart Planner Matching Engine under real-world conditions.
- Evaluate the AI-Assisted Event Design Canvas and collaborative design workflow.

- Generates practical insights into user behavior and marketplace dynamics.

# 8. Business Model

Following successful product validation and market adoption, Celebrate will operate as a multi-sided Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) marketplace connecting **Clients**, **Event Management Teams**, and **Event Professionals**. The platform generates recurring revenue through planner subscriptions, event professional subscriptions, and a success-based commission earned only when a confirmed booking is secured through the platform.

Celebrate follows an ethical marketplace approach where **clients never pay any subscription fee or platform transaction fee**, ensuring a frictionless booking experience while monetization is driven entirely by professionals who derive business value from the platform.

### **Advance Payment Success Commission**

Celebrate earns a **one-time success commission only when a client confirms a booking by paying the advance payment through the platform**.

The commission model follows a transparent and ethical approach:

- Commission is charged **only once**.
- Commission is deducted only from the **Advance Payment**.
- **No commission is charged on the Final Payment.**
- Celebrate does not earn additional revenue after the booking has been successfully confirmed.
- Client ratings are collected after the event is completed rather than being linked to payment completion.

The recommended commission rate is:

**15% of the Advance Payment**

This approach ensures Celebrate is compensated only for successfully matching a client with an event management team while maintaining fairness and transparency for planners.

### **Revenue Model Rationale**

Celebrate operates as a **Business-to-Business (B2B) SaaS Marketplace** where event management companies and event professionals fund the platform through recurring subscriptions, while Celebrate also earns a success-based commission only after delivering a confirmed client booking.

Unlike traditional marketplaces that charge commissions throughout the payment lifecycle, Celebrate earns revenue only once—at the Advance Payment stage—because this represents the point at which the platform has successfully fulfilled its core responsibility of matching a qualified client with an event management team. No commission is charged on the Final Payment, promoting transparency, trust, and long-term planner relationships.

Planner subscriptions are positioned as access to the Celebrate Planner Operating System rather than simply AI image generation. Subscribers gain qualified lead allocation, intelligent planner matching, proposal management, AI-assisted design creation, portfolio management, recruitment visibility, and business management tools, making the subscription valuable regardless of AI credit consumption.

Clients remain permanently free to use the platform, eliminating adoption barriers and encouraging marketplace growth. This multi-sided revenue strategy combines predictable recurring subscription income with scalable success-based commission revenue, creating a sustainable marketplace that grows alongside its expanding ecosystem of clients, planners, and event professionals.
